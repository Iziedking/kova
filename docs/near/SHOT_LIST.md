# Demo video and X post: shot list

For Benita. One video of about 90 seconds, screen recorded on https://near.kova.surf, plus one X
post. Everything shown must be live; do not stage a result.

## Before recording

1. A fresh NEAR testnet wallet (MyNearWallet or Meteor) with about 1 testnet NEAR from the faucet,
   signed out of near.kova.surf.
2. Open https://near.kova.surf/play and look at "Beside the launchpad". Decide which round to film:
   - **If an auction ends within 14 minutes**, film a "Will it cook?" call (shot 4a). The picker
     shows "<SYMBOL> cooks" and "<SYMBOL> does not cook" only inside that window.
   - **Otherwise** film a launch pick: JAMBO or PURGE from "Launched on meme.cooking" with a 5 min
     round (shot 4b). These coins trade a few times an hour, so a short round often ties at 0.
3. Browser at 1440 wide, zoom 100%, dark theme. A second clip at phone width (390) for shot 2 is a
   bonus, not required.

into the compose box; if it shows an old card, add `?v=2` to the link.
