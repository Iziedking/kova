# Kova demo video: the script you record

Story first, then the product. About 2 minutes 20 seconds. You read the **Voiceover** lines in your own voice;
the motion graphics and screen recordings are built around your recording.

Everything said here is true on NEAR testnet today. Two lines change depending on the AI key (scene 6); read the
one that matches what the site shows on the day.

**How to record your voice**
- Quiet room, phone or mic about a hand's width from your mouth. Record as WAV or the best quality your app allows.
- One file per scene, named `scene-01.wav`, `scene-02.wav` and so on. Leave one second of silence at the start and end.
- Talk like you are explaining it to a friend, not reading. If a line feels stiff, change the words; keep the facts.
- If you stumble, pause and say the whole line again. We cut the bad take.

## Scene 5. Play a round (0:45 to 1:25)

**Screen recording, step by step:**
1. Sign in, press "Approve once and get 1,000 CHIP". Show the CHIP balance.
2. Open the CHIP mine. Pick a square. Press "Put 10 CHIP on square 13". No wallet popup.
3. The timer runs out. Show "Last round": the winning square, the payout, the burn.

**Voiceover:**
"I sign in and approve once. That gives me a game key that can only stake CHIP. It can't move my coins.
Now I pick a square and stake. No wallet popup.
When the minute ends, the winning square comes from NEAR's own random number. Not from us.
Winners get their stake back, a share of the losing coins, and fresh CHIP. And ten percent of the losing coins is burned. Every round."

**On-screen text (one at a time):**
Approve once
No popups
Random from NEAR
10% burned, every round

**Movement:** Gentle zoom on the button press, then on the result.

**Transition:** Quick cross-fade.

## Do not say

- Mainnet, real money, or an audit.
- That a meme.cooking coin already has a Kova mine. It is planned.
- That the AI wrote a recap, unless the site shows "AI status: live" on the day.
- That Kova is partnered with or affiliated with meme.cooking. It is not.
