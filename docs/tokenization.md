Prepared 2026-09-19 from the official AnsemHack and ClawPump Partner API pages. This is a review checklist, not a launch request, wallet authorization or token promise.

- Post the generated announcement on X and follow `@clawpumptech` through the official flow.
- Tokenize on ClawPump or EasyA by **1 October 2026, 24:00 EST (UTC-5)** and verify with the same registered X handle.
- KOVA is choosing the ClawPump route; do not also represent it as an EasyA entry.

Recheck the official page immediately before every owner action because dates, fields and launch economics can change.

## Prepared project copy

**Project name:** KOVA

**One line:** KOVA is an evidence-gated multiplayer game where an AI Dealer investigates unfamiliar Solana stock-themed meme tokens, while deterministic code scores picks and user-signed ANSEM escrow remains recoverable without the agent.

**Agent role:** The KOVA Dealer decides whether an exact submitted mint has enough evidence to enter. It does not choose picks, prices, winners, transactions or payouts.

**Current product utility:** ANSEM is the proposed equal-stake game asset, subject to exact identity, legal and deployment gates. No implemented KOVA-token utility exists yet. Do not claim governance, fee sharing, access, yield, buybacks or revenue rights unless that utility is separately designed, implemented, reviewed and reflected in the product.

**Ticker:** owner decision required. `KOVA` is a proposal, not a reserved or launched ticker.

**Website:** owner must supply the reviewed live URL.

**Image URL:** owner must supply a public HTTPS asset after brand review.

**Token link/mint:** leave blank until the owner completes a verified launch. Never prefill a guessed mint.

## Owner launch review

1. Confirm the registered project X handle, account ownership and team/contact fields.
2. Confirm token name, ticker, description, image, website and whether any utility statement is actually implemented.
3. Read the exact current ClawPump launch quote, creator fee, pair choice, wallet source and transaction message.
4. Keep `initialBuySol=0` unless the owner separately authorizes a specific buy amount after reviewing all costs and risks.
5. The owner signs and pays. The backend, Dealer and this repository must not launch or buy the token autonomously.
6. Record the launch receipt, mint, network, transaction signature, agent/account identity and official entry attachment.
7. Verify the public token page and official AnsemHack entry independently before publishing claims.

## Proof bundle after owner actions

- clean `kova-release-manifest-v1` output and Git revision;
- live frontend/API URLs and capability response;
- exact Dealer evidence/refusal receipt with no unsafe tool invocation;
- approved-network ANSEM deposit, deterministic result and payout receipt;
- separate timeout/refund receipt while Dealer/backend is unavailable;
- token launch signature and official entry attachment;
- X announcement URL from the registered identity;
- explicit limitations, network labels and provenance for pre-existing versus new work.

Until those receipts exist, describe KOVA as a locally verified preview architecture, not a live game or completed hackathon entry.
